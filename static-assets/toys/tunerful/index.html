The pen rides along the cents scale at the foot of the chart, and the paper feeds upwards while a note sounds, so the sheet holds your tuning history for the current string. A new sheet starts whenever the string changes. Tap a string to lock the tuner to it and turn off auto-detect. The green band is ±3 cents. The orange mark on the input bar is the noise gate, which rises with background noise.

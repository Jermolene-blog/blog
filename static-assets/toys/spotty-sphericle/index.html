Spotty Sphericle

Dots on a sphere, or a cube, a doughnut, a rippling water drop or a cloud, sized by Lambert light. Flick the image to send it spinning, or switch to light mode and drag to move the light.

Explore

Shows the current image in the centre with eight nearby variations around it. Click a variation to make it the new centre and breed again, or click the centre to leave explore. Sliders below still edit the centre, and the neighbours follow.

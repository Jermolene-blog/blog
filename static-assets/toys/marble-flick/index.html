Marble Flick

Gather round the board. Take turns flicking marbles into the rings, and knock your rivals into the hole.

Drag back from your marble and let go to flick it. When everything stops, marbles in the rings score 1, 3 and 5. Sinking a rival's marble in the centre hole earns +3. Anything that rolls off the edge is gone, and a marble left sitting on a launch pad blows up and costs the shooter 2. The four hotspots on the edges swallow any marble that rolls slowly across them and set something off: an earthquake, a spin surge, a hungry snake or a mischievous cat. Handy when you're losing. Each one needs a full round to recharge, and after every event the hotspots shuffle places. Every third marble is a giant: heavy enough to bulldoze, too big for the hole, and it shrinks back once everything stops. Tap a seat to switch it between player, computer and empty.

## Fixes
- Rumours, Running Up That Hill, Ziggy Stardust, Dark Side of the Moon, Live and Let Die, A View to a Kill, A Design for Life: took out the year. The year added nothing and couldn't be checked within the search budget; each question still has one answer without it.
- Cliff Richard "original backing group": a pedant could answer The Drifters (the Shadows' first name). Now "Which group backed Cliff Richard on 1963's 'Summer Holiday'?" (credited to Cliff Richard and the Shadows): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_UK_singles_chart_number_ones_of_the_1960s
- FGTH: "Relax" came out in 1983, so the old wording was wrong. Now "first three singles all reached number one in 1984" (Jan, Jun and Dec 1984): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_UK_singles_chart_number_ones_of_the_1980s
- Mary Hopkin "first came to notice": she had already released a Welsh-language EP. Now "found fame by winning which TV talent show": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Hopkin
- Festive Fifty "compiled by": it was voted by listeners and later presented by other DJs. Now "listener-voted ... began in 1976 on which Radio 1 DJ's show": https://wiki.riteme.site/wiki/Festive_50
- Rod Stewart "before going solo": his solo career ran alongside the Faces from 1969, and before that he sang with the Jeff Beck Group. Now "From 1969 to 1975, Rod Stewart was also lead singer of which band?": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Faces

## Fixes
- Penguin colours: crime fiction was green, so "Green" could be argued as a fiction colour. Now "for general fiction".
- Ye Olde: the source says printers used y for thorn "or the eth", so the Eth distractor was arguable. Swapped Eth for Ash.
- Wild-goose chase: the earliest recorded use is Markham's 1593 horsemanship treatise, so "the earliest recorded ... is in which Shakespeare play" was false as worded. Now "The earliest figurative 'wild-goose chase' in print is in which Shakespeare play?" (Romeo and Juliet, 1597 quarto).
- Penguin founder: Allen Lane co-founded Penguin with his brothers Richard and John, as an imprint of The Bodley Head. Now "Which publisher, with his brothers, launched Penguin paperbacks in 1935?"

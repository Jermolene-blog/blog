## Fixes
- Wiltshire: Wikipedia lists "Trowbridge, Salisbury or Devizes" and the council says the county "never had a well recognised county town". Reworded to "Wiltshire Council has its headquarters in which town?" (Trowbridge; Swindon is a separate unitary authority).
- Somerset: Wikipedia lists "Taunton, Ilchester, Bath or Wells". Replaced distractors Bath and Wells with Bridgwater and Frome.
- Kent: Canterbury hosted East Kent sessions. Replaced Canterbury with Dover.
- Suffolk: Bury St Edmunds was West Suffolk's HQ 1889 to 1974. Replaced with Felixstowe.
- Channel Tunnel: the tunnel itself does not "run to" a commune (portal and Sangatte shaft are separate from the terminal). Reworded to ask for the French terminal's commune.
- Rutland: Isle of Wight (380 km2) is smaller than Rutland and is now a county. Replaced the distractor with Middlesex.
